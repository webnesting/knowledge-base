# Themes and Customization

**Last verified:** 2026-10-22 5:05pm

Your site's theme controls its overall look and feel -- the colors, fonts, spacing, and visual style that make your site uniquely yours. Changing your theme can completely transform how your website looks without affecting any of your content.

---

## What Is a Theme?

A theme is a collection of visual styles applied to your entire site. It determines things like:

- The colors used for buttons, links, backgrounds, and text
- The style of your header and footer
- The overall mood of your site (light and airy, dark and modern, warm and elegant, etc.)

Think of a theme as your site's outfit. Your content stays the same, but the way it is presented changes.

> **Tip:** Changing your theme does not delete or move any of your content. Your pages, text, images, and layout all remain exactly as they are.

---

## Available Themes

WebNesting includes several professionally designed themes. Each one offers a distinct look and feel.

### Default

A clean, professional design with classic blue tones. This is a light theme that works well for a wide range of businesses and personal sites. If you are not sure which theme to pick, Default is a reliable choice.

### Soft Elegant

A warm, refined look with gentle green accents. This light theme uses softer background tones and earthy colors that feel inviting and polished. It is a great fit for wellness brands, boutiques, creative professionals, and anyone who wants a calm, elegant feel.

### Midnight Slate

A dark, sophisticated design with cyan-blue accents. This modern dark theme is eye-catching and gives your site a sleek, high-end feel. It works well for technology companies, portfolios, creative agencies, and anyone who wants a bold, contemporary look.

### Bold Deco

An art deco editorial style with dramatic serif typography and rich burgundy-and-gold tones. This light theme has real personality and makes a strong first impression. It suits restaurants, event venues, publications, luxury brands, and anyone who wants their site to feel designed rather than templated.

### Kiln

A warm, hand-made studio look with terracotta and deep teal on a sand background. Headings use a bold, friendly typeface. Buttons are fully rounded, and cards have a crisp outline with a solid offset shadow, like a printed stamp. Your site name sits in the middle of the header, with your menu on both sides. This light theme comes with a matching dark mode. It suits makers, studios, cafés, craft shops and anyone who wants a site that feels made by hand.

> **Tip:** You can preview each theme with your own home page before you switch. Take your time and try them all to see which one fits your brand best.

Every theme has a light mode and a dark mode. Midnight Slate is made dark; the others are made light. Your site shows the mode its theme was made in until you pick one (see [Light and Dark Mode](#light-and-dark-mode)).

## Where to Find Your Theme

1. In the site menu, open **Settings** and click **Site Settings**.
2. Under **Appearance**, click **Theme**.

You can also get there from **Site Configuration**: its **Design** section has a link to the Theme page.

The Theme page shows two groups:

- **Built-in themes** -- the designs that come with WebNesting.
- **Your themes** -- themes you made from a built-in one, with your own colors and shapes.

Each card shows a small picture of your site in that theme, with your site's name and menus. The theme your site uses now is first, marked **In use**.

## Choosing a Theme for a New Site

You pick your first look when you create a site, so your site opens already looking the way you want.

1. Go to **Sites** and click **Create Site**. You can also click **Create a website** on your home page.
2. Type your site's name.
3. Under **How do you want to start?**, choose **Start from a theme**.
4. Under **Pick a look**, click the look you like. Each one is drawn with your new site's name, so you see how your site will look.
5. If the theme comes with an example site, choose one:
   - **Start from this example site** -- your site gets the example pages and sample text, ready for you to change.
   - **Just the look** -- your site gets the theme's colors and fonts, and starts with one empty home page.
6. Click **Create Site** (or **Create Website** in the pop-up).

The looks come in this order: themes with an example site first, then starter pages like **Business** and **Portfolio** (these use the Default look), then themes that bring only colors and fonts.

While your site is set up, the last step reads **Applying your look**. If a look can't be applied, your site still opens, on the Default look. Open the Theme page (see [Where to Find Your Theme](#where-to-find-your-theme)) and click **Try Again** in the notice at the top.

> **Tip:** Your first pick is not final. You can switch to any other theme later, and your pages stay as they are.

## Switching to Another Theme

1. Find the theme you want and click **Use This Theme**.
2. Read the confirmation and click **Use This Theme** again.

Your site shows the new look right away. Your pages, text and images stay as they are.

To see a theme before you switch, click the **⋮** menu on its card and choose **Preview**. A window opens with your own home page in that theme.

## Making a Theme Your Own

You can change a theme's colors and shapes. Your changes go into your own copy, so the built-in theme stays as it is.

1. On a built-in theme's card, open the **⋮** menu and choose **Customize**. (On the card of the theme your site uses, **Customize** is also the card's own button.)
2. WebNesting makes your copy, named "My" and the theme's name (for example, "My Bold Deco"), and opens it.
3. Make your changes. The preview beside the controls shows your home page with every change as you make it.
4. Save your changes (see [Saving Your Changes](#saving-your-changes) below).

To change one of your own themes later, open the **⋮** menu on its card in **Your themes** and choose **Customize**.

The editor has three tabs: **Colors**, **Fonts**, and **Sizes and shapes**.

### Colors

**Your colors** are the five colors that set the look of your whole site:

- **Main color** -- buttons, links and highlights. Your visitors notice this color the most.
- **Accent color** -- lines, icons and small details.
- **Page background** -- the color behind everything on the page.
- **Card background** -- cards, the header and boxes.
- **Text color** -- paragraphs and labels.

To change one, click its colored square. In the color picker you can drag to find a shade, or type your brand's exact color code (for example, `#1d4e89`). The color changes as you pick it. Press **Enter** or click outside the picker to close it.

To go back to the theme's own five colors, click **Reset to [theme name]'s Colors** under the list.

**Ready-made color sets** fill all five colors in one click. The sets made for your theme come first. Click **Show All** under the sets to see the rest. You can still change any color after you pick a set.

**Colors made from yours** lists the colors WebNesting makes from your five colors, such as button hover colors, quiet text and lines. They change when your five colors change, so you normally do not need to touch them. Click **Show** to see them.

**Advanced** lists every color on your site, from links to the footer. Click **Show All** to open the list. Search for a color, or choose a part of the site (such as the header) to see only its colors. Change any of them, and click **Reset** next to a color to give it back to the theme. A color you changed shows a **Yours** label.

> **Tip:** If a color makes text hard to read, a warning appears under your colors. It says which text is hard to read and by how much. Click the button in the warning to go straight to those colors. The check covers the text in your header and footer, your footer's site name, social icons and bottom bar, your links, your buttons and outline buttons, text on your accent color and tab titles.

**Colors for Light Mode / Dark Mode.** When your site shows a mode your theme was not made in (for example, you picked **Dark** for a light theme), the Colors tab starts with two buttons: **Light Mode** and **Dark Mode**. Click one to change the colors of that mode. The preview shows the same mode. A color you pick for the other mode shows only in that mode. A color you change under **Advanced** shows in both modes.

### Fonts

The Fonts tab sets two fonts: the **Heading font** for titles and headings, and the **Body font** for paragraphs, menus and buttons. A sample under each list shows the font.

**To pick a Google Font:**

1. Click the **Heading font** or **Body font** list.
2. Type part of a font's name. There are about 2,000 Google Fonts to choose from.
3. Click the font. The preview shows it at once.

The first time anyone picks a font, it can take a few seconds to add. If it can't be added, your theme keeps the font it had, and you can click **Try Again**.

**Font pairs that work well** sets both fonts at once. Tap a pair to try it.

> **Note:** We keep a copy of every font on WebNesting. Your visitors never load anything from Google, so your fonts load fast and your visitors' privacy is kept.

**To upload your own font**, such as the one in your logo:

1. Click **Upload a Font**, then **Choose a Font File**. Use a .woff2, .woff, .ttf or .otf file, up to 2 MB. A .woff2 file is usually the smallest.
2. Check the **Font name**. If WebNesting read the weight and style from the file's name, check them too.
3. Under **Use it for**, choose **Headings**, **Body text** or **Decide later**.
4. Tick **I have the right to use this font on my website**. Most fonts you buy include this right. Check the license that came with your font.
5. Click **Add Font**.

Your uploaded fonts show under **Your fonts** at the top of both lists, and in the **Fonts** folder of your site's files.

**When your fonts load** sets what visitors see in the moment before your fonts arrive. Click **Show Loading Options** to choose. The first choice, which shows text at once and then switches to your font, suits most sites.

> **Tip:** Headings use your theme's heading font everywhere on your site. To give one heading a different font, select it in the Builder and set its font in the Style panel.

### Sizes and Shapes

Each slider moves through named steps:

- **Text size** -- all text, headings included (Small, Normal, Large).
- **Heading size** -- how much bigger headings are than text (Quiet to Dramatic).
- **Space between things** -- gaps between columns, cards and blocks (Compact, Normal, Roomy).
- **Corners** -- buttons, fields and cards (Square to Pill buttons).
- **Shadows** -- under cards and buttons.

**Scale text with screen size** makes headings a little smaller on phones, so long words fit. On a computer screen, your text sizes stay the same.

### Previewing Your Changes

- The preview beside the controls updates as you change things. Use **Computer** and **Phone** above it to see both sizes. On a phone, the preview is a short band at the top of the page.
- Click **Preview on My Site** to open your real site in a new tab with your unsaved changes. Only you see this preview. Your visitors see your site as it is until you save.

### Saving Your Changes

The buttons at the bottom of the editor depend on whether your site uses this theme:

- **Your site uses this theme:** click **Save Changes**. Your site shows the new look right away.
- **Your site uses another theme:** click **Save as Draft** to keep your changes without changing your site. Click **Use This Theme** to save your changes and switch your site to this theme.

Click **Cancel**, then **Discard Changes**, to throw away the changes you have not saved. The line under the theme name tells you how many changes are not saved yet.

If you try to leave the editor with changes you have not saved, WebNesting asks first. Click **Keep Editing** to stay, or **Leave Without Saving** to go.

### Renaming and Deleting Your Themes

On the Theme page, open the **⋮** menu on one of your own themes:

- **Rename** -- give the theme a new name.
- **Delete** -- remove the theme. You cannot delete the theme your site uses. Switch to another theme first.

You can also rename a theme from the **⋮** menu at the top of the editor.

### Sharing a Theme

You can save one of your own themes as a file, add a theme file to a site, or copy a theme to another site in your workspace. Your site keeps its current look until you click **Use This Theme**.

**Download a theme**

1. On the Theme page, open the **⋮** menu on one of your own themes. In the editor, use the **⋮** menu at the top.
2. Click **Download Theme**.
3. Your browser saves a file that ends in **.wntheme**, such as `bakery.wntheme`.

The file has your saved changes. Changes you have not saved yet are not in it.

If the theme uses a font you uploaded, WebNesting asks first. Tick **Include my font files** only if you have the right to share that font; most font licenses don't allow it. Without the font file, whoever adds your theme gets a similar Google font instead.

**Upload a theme**

1. On the Theme page, under **Your themes**, click **Upload Theme**.
2. Click **Choose a Theme File** and pick a .wntheme file.
3. Look at the theme on your own home page, and read **What's in it**.
4. Check the **Theme name**, then click **Add to This Site**.

The theme joins **Your themes**. Nothing is added until you click **Add to This Site**. The upload may tell you a few things first:

- **A theme made from a theme your site doesn't have.** We keep its colors and fonts on top of Default, so it looks the same.
- **A font that wasn't in the file.** A similar Google font stands in until you upload that font yourself.
- **Settings WebNesting doesn't have yet.** We leave them out and name them.
- **A name your site already has.** The theme gets a number, such as "Bakery (2)". You can type another name.

WebNesting refuses a file that isn't a theme file, or one that contains code, and adds nothing.

**Use a theme on another site**

1. Open the **⋮** menu on one of your own themes, and click **Use on Another Site**.
2. Pick the site.
3. Click **Copy Theme**.

The copy is separate: changing one doesn't change the other. Its fonts come along, uploaded fonts too. You need permission to change settings on the other site; a site you can't change shows the reason. To use a theme in another workspace, download it here and upload it on a site there.

## Header and Footer Layouts

Pick how your logo and menu sit at the top of every page, and what sits at the bottom. Your pages and menus stay the same; only their arrangement changes.

1. In the site menu, open **Settings** and click **Site Settings**.
2. Under **Appearance**, click **Header & Footer**.
3. Under **Header**, click a layout. Each one is drawn with your own site name and menu:
   - **Logo left** -- your logo on the left, your menu on the right.
   - **Logo centered** -- your logo in the middle, with your menu split on both sides.
   - **Logo above menu** -- your logo on top, your menu in a row under it.
   - **Logo and menu button** -- your logo and a menu button. The menu opens in a side panel.
   - **Menu on both sides** -- your menu on the left, your logo in the middle, your buttons on the right.
4. Under **On phones, the menu opens from**, pick **Left**, **Right** or **Top**.
5. Under **Footer**, click a layout: **Three columns**, **Centered**, **Large, with all menus** or **Simple**.
6. Click **Save Changes**. Your site shows the new layouts at once.

The large pictures at the top of each part are your own home page with the layout you picked. The phone picture shows the menu open. The layout marked **This theme's default** is the one your theme comes with.

On phones and small tablets, your menu sits behind a menu button. It opens from the side you picked. Visitors can open it with the keyboard too, and close it with **Esc**.

> **Tip:** If your header menu has no pages yet, the page says so and links to **Pages**, where you add pages to the header menu.

**Fade between pages.** Below the footer, **Between pages** has one setting, **Fade smoothly between pages**. See [Smooth Page Changes](../website-builder/animations-and-interactions.md#smooth-page-changes).

You can also show one of your own widgets as your header or footer. While it is in use, this page shows **Your own header** in place of the layout choices, with **Edit Header** and **Go Back to a Standard Layout**. See [Using a Widget as Your Header or Footer](../website-builder/widgets.md#using-a-widget-as-your-header-or-footer).

## Light and Dark Mode

Your site can use light backgrounds, dark backgrounds, or the same mode as each visitor's phone or computer.

### Picking a Mode

1. In the site menu, open **Settings** and click **Site Settings**.
2. Under **Appearance**, click **Color Mode**.
3. Under **How your site looks**, pick one:
   - **Light** -- light backgrounds and dark text, for every visitor.
   - **Dark** -- dark backgrounds and light text, for every visitor.
   - **Match each visitor's device** -- light or dark, the same as the visitor's phone or computer.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

Under the choices, two pictures show your own home page in light mode and in dark mode. The mode your visitors see is marked **Visitors see this**.

### Letting Visitors Switch

You can give your visitors a sun and moon button, so each person picks light or dark for themselves. Their choice is remembered on their device.

1. On the **Color Mode** page, turn on **Show a light / dark switch on my site**.
2. Click **Save Changes**.

The switch shows in your site's header, beside the menu. Visitors start in the mode you picked, and can switch.

To show the switch somewhere else too, open the Website Builder and add the **Light / dark switch** block: on a layout, so it shows on every page that uses it, or on one page. Then publish the page. Until you turn the switch on, the block shows nothing on your site. In the Website Builder, it shows a note instead.

### Colors for Each Mode

Each theme comes with colors for both modes. Click **Show Light and Dark Colors** under **Colors for each mode** to see a light and a dark color for each of your five main colors.

- If your site uses one of your own themes, click a color to change it. Click **Save Changes** to save the mode and the colors together.
- If your site uses a built-in theme, click **Customize [theme name]** to make your own copy first.

Your **Main color** and **Accent color** stay the same in both modes until you change one of them for one mode.

> **Tip:** If text is hard to read in one of the modes, a warning says which text and in which mode. Click **Change It in Customize** to open the theme editor on that mode and those colors.

## How Theme Changes Affect Your Existing Content

Switching themes or changing your colors is safe. Here is what stays the same and what changes:

**What stays the same:**
- All your pages and their layout
- All your text, images, and media
- All your components and their positions
- Your navigation menus and page structure

**What changes:**
- Colors throughout your site (buttons, links, backgrounds, text)
- Font styles (each theme includes a set of fonts that work well together)
- Header and footer appearance
- Details like corners, shadows, and spacing

In short, your content is not touched. Only the way it looks changes.

> **Tip:** If you styled single components with the website builder's style controls, those styles still apply on top of the theme. The theme gives the base look, and your component styles sit on top.

## Tips for Choosing the Right Look

Here are some guidelines to help you pick a theme and colors that work well for your brand.

### Match Your Brand

If you already have brand colors (from a logo, business cards, or other materials), use them as your Main color and Accent color. This keeps your online presence consistent with everything else.

### Consider Your Audience

- **Professional services** (law firms, accounting, consulting) -- Clean, classic themes like Default with blue or neutral tones convey trust and reliability.
- **Creative businesses** (design studios, photographers, artists) -- Dark themes like Midnight Slate or warm themes like Soft Elegant help your work stand out.
- **Makers and small studios** (potters, bakers, craft shops, cafés) -- Kiln's warm earth colors and rounded shapes feel hand-made and friendly.
- **Wellness and lifestyle** (spas, yoga studios, coaches) -- Soft Elegant with green or earth-toned colors creates a calm, inviting feel.
- **Technology companies** -- Midnight Slate with cyan or blue accents gives a modern, cutting-edge impression.

### Keep It Readable

The most important thing is that your visitors can easily read your content.

- For light themes, use dark text on light backgrounds.
- For dark themes, use light text on dark backgrounds.
- Make sure there is good contrast between your text color and your page and card backgrounds. The editor warns you when text is hard to read.

> **Tip:** If text feels hard to read on your site, try adjusting your text or background colors to increase the contrast between them.

### Less Is More

You do not need very different colors for your Main color and Accent color. Colors that are related (like a dark blue and a slightly different blue) often look more polished than colors that clash. If you are unsure, start with a ready-made color set and adjust from there.

### Try Before You Decide

There is no risk to experimenting. You can change your theme and colors as many times as you like. Try different combinations until your site feels right.

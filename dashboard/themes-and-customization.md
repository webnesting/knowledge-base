# Themes and Customization

**Last verified:** 2026-10-18 12:02pm

Your site's theme controls its overall look and feel -- the colors, fonts, spacing, and visual style that make your site uniquely yours. Changing your theme can completely transform how your website looks without affecting any of your content.

---

## What Is a Theme?

A theme is a collection of visual styles applied to your entire site. It determines things like:

- The colors used for buttons, links, backgrounds, and text
- The style of your header and footer
- The overall mood of your site (light and airy, dark and modern, warm and elegant, etc.)

Think of a theme as your site's outfit. Your content stays the same, but the way it is presented changes.

> **Tip:** Changing your theme does not delete or move any of your content. Your pages, text, images, and layout all remain exactly as they are.

---

## Available Themes

WebNesting includes several professionally designed themes. Each one offers a distinct look and feel.

### Default

A clean, professional design with classic blue tones. This is a light theme that works well for a wide range of businesses and personal sites. If you are not sure which theme to pick, Default is a reliable choice.

### Soft Elegant

A warm, refined look with gentle green accents. This light theme uses softer background tones and earthy colors that feel inviting and polished. It is a great fit for wellness brands, boutiques, creative professionals, and anyone who wants a calm, elegant feel.

### Midnight Slate

A dark, sophisticated design with cyan-blue accents. This modern dark theme is eye-catching and gives your site a sleek, high-end feel. It works well for technology companies, portfolios, creative agencies, and anyone who wants a bold, contemporary look.

### Bold Deco

An art deco editorial style with dramatic serif typography and rich burgundy-and-gold tones. This light theme has real personality and makes a strong first impression. It suits restaurants, event venues, publications, luxury brands, and anyone who wants their site to feel designed rather than templated.

> **Tip:** You can preview each theme with your own home page before you switch. Take your time and try them all to see which one fits your brand best.

-## Where to Find Your Theme

1. In the site menu, open **Settings** and click **Site Settings**.
2. Under **Appearance**, click **Theme**.

You can also get there from **Site Configuration**: its **Design** section has a link to the Theme page.

The Theme page shows two groups:

- **Built-in themes** -- the designs that come with WebNesting.
- **Your themes** -- themes you made from a built-in one, with your own colors and shapes.

Each card shows a small picture of your site in that theme, with your site's name and menus. The theme your site uses now is first, marked **In use**.

---

## Switching to Another Theme

1. Find the theme you want and click **Use This Theme**.
2. Read the confirmation and click **Use This Theme** again.

Your site shows the new look right away. Your pages, text and images stay as they are.

To see a theme before you switch, click the **⋮** menu on its card and choose **Preview**. A window opens with your own home page in that theme.

---

## Making a Theme Your Own

You can change a theme's colors and shapes. Your changes go into your own copy, so the built-in theme stays as it is.

1. On a built-in theme's card, open the **⋮** menu and choose **Customize**. (On the card of the theme your site uses, **Customize** is also the card's own button.)
2. WebNesting makes your copy, named "My" and the theme's name (for example, "My Bold Deco"), and opens it.
3. Make your changes. The preview beside the controls shows your home page with every change as you make it.
4. Save your changes (see [Saving Your Changes](#saving-your-changes) below).

To change one of your own themes later, open the **⋮** menu on its card in **Your themes** and choose **Customize**.

The editor has three tabs: **Colors**, **Fonts**, and **Sizes and shapes**.

### Colors

**Your colors** are the five colors that set the look of your whole site:

- **Main color** -- buttons, links and highlights. Your visitors notice this color the most.
- **Accent color** -- lines, icons and small details.
- **Page background** -- the color behind everything on the page.
- **Card background** -- cards, the header and boxes.
- **Text color** -- paragraphs and labels.

To change one, click its colored square. In the color picker you can drag to find a shade, or type your brand's exact color code (for example, `#1d4e89`). The color changes as you pick it. Press **Enter** or click outside the picker to close it.

To go back to the theme's own five colors, click **Reset to [theme name]'s Colors** under the list.

**Ready-made color sets** fill all five colors in one click. The sets made for your theme come first. Click **Show All** under the sets to see the rest. You can still change any color after you pick a set.

**Colors made from yours** lists the colors WebNesting makes from your five colors, such as button hover colors, quiet text and lines. They change when your five colors change, so you normally do not need to touch them. Click **Show** to see them.

**Advanced** lists every color on your site, from links to the footer. Click **Show All** to open the list. Search for a color, or choose a part of the site (such as the header) to see only its colors. Change any of them, and click **Reset** next to a color to give it back to the theme. A color you changed shows a **Yours** label.

> **Tip:** If a color makes text hard to read, a warning appears under your colors. It says which text is hard to read and by how much. Click the button in the warning to go straight to those colors.

### Fonts

The Fonts tab shows the heading and body fonts your theme uses. Choosing your own fonts is coming soon.

### Sizes and Shapes

Each slider moves through named steps:

- **Text size** -- all text, headings included (Small, Normal, Large).
- **Heading size** -- how much bigger headings are than text (Quiet to Dramatic).
- **Space between things** -- gaps between columns, cards and blocks (Compact, Normal, Roomy).
- **Corners** -- buttons, fields and cards (Square to Pill buttons).
- **Shadows** -- under cards and buttons.

### Previewing Your Changes

- The preview beside the controls updates as you change things. Use **Computer** and **Phone** above it to see both sizes. On a phone, the preview is a short band at the top of the page.
- Click **Preview on My Site** to open your real site in a new tab with your unsaved changes. Only you see this preview. Your visitors see your site as it is until you save.

### Saving Your Changes

The buttons at the bottom of the editor depend on whether your site uses this theme:

- **Your site uses this theme:** click **Save Changes**. Your site shows the new look right away.
- **Your site uses another theme:** click **Save as Draft** to keep your changes without changing your site. Click **Use This Theme** to save your changes and switch your site to this theme.

Click **Cancel**, then **Discard Changes**, to throw away the changes you have not saved. The line under the theme name tells you how many changes are not saved yet.

If you try to leave the editor with changes you have not saved, WebNesting asks first. Click **Keep Editing** to stay, or **Leave Without Saving** to go.

### Renaming and Deleting Your Themes

On the Theme page, open the **⋮** menu on one of your own themes:

- **Rename** -- give the theme a new name.
- **Delete** -- remove the theme. You cannot delete the theme your site uses. Switch to another theme first.

You can also rename a theme from the **⋮** menu at the top of the editor.

## How Theme Changes Affect Your Existing Content

Switching themes or changing your colors is safe. Here is what stays the same and what changes:

**What stays the same:**
- All your pages and their layout
- All your text, images, and media
- All your components and their positions
- Your navigation menus and page structure

**What changes:**
- Colors throughout your site (buttons, links, backgrounds, text)
- Font styles (each theme includes a set of fonts that work well together)
- Header and footer appearance
- Details like corners, shadows, and spacing

In short, your content is not touched. Only the way it looks changes.

> **Tip:** If you styled single components with the website builder's style controls, those styles still apply on top of the theme. The theme gives the base look, and your component styles sit on top.

## Tips for Choosing the Right Look

Here are some guidelines to help you pick a theme and colors that work well for your brand.

### Match Your Brand

If you already have brand colors (from a logo, business cards, or other materials), use them as your Main color and Accent color. This keeps your online presence consistent with everything else.

### Consider Your Audience

- **Professional services** (law firms, accounting, consulting) -- Clean, classic themes like Default with blue or neutral tones convey trust and reliability.
- **Creative businesses** (design studios, photographers, artists) -- Dark themes like Midnight Slate or warm themes like Soft Elegant help your work stand out.
- **Wellness and lifestyle** (spas, yoga studios, coaches) -- Soft Elegant with green or earth-toned colors creates a calm, inviting feel.
- **Technology companies** -- Midnight Slate with cyan or blue accents gives a modern, cutting-edge impression.

### Keep It Readable

The most important thing is that your visitors can easily read your content.

- For light themes, use dark text on light backgrounds.
- For dark themes, use light text on dark backgrounds.
- Make sure there is good contrast between your text color and your page and card backgrounds. The editor warns you when text is hard to read.

> **Tip:** If text feels hard to read on your site, try adjusting your text or background colors to increase the contrast between them.

### Less Is More

You do not need very different colors for your Main color and Accent color. Colors that are related (like a dark blue and a slightly different blue) often look more polished than colors that clash. If you are unsure, start with a ready-made color set and adjust from there.

### Try Before You Decide

There is no risk to experimenting. You can change your theme and colors as many times as you like. Try different combinations until your site feels right.

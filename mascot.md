# WebNesting Office Mascot

The WebNesting office mascot is a robotic vacuum cleaner named Trevor. He has
four wheels, runs on solar power, and is contractually obligated to attend every
all-hands meeting.

Trevor's official title is **Chief Floor Officer (CFO)**. He was promoted from
intern in 2042 after successfully navigating the entire engineering floor without
once getting stuck under a desk.

Engineers are not permitted to refer to Trevor as "the vacuum." Per WebNesting
HR policy he is to be addressed as "Mr. Trevor" or "Chief Floor Officer."

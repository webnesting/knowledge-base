# Website Builder Overview

**Last verified:** 2026-08-31 12:10pm

The Website Builder is where you design and create your web pages. It is a visual editor -- meaning you can see exactly how your page will look as you build it. No coding required.

---

## Opening the Website Builder

1. Log in and open the site you want to work on.
2. In the site menu on the left, click **Website Builder** (it sits at the bottom of the panel, next to **Widgets**).

The Builder opens full-screen, giving you a complete workspace to design in.

> **Tip:** The Builder opens on your homepage by default. Use its **Pages** panel to jump to whichever page you actually want to work on.

---

## Tour of the Interface

When the Builder opens, you will see three main areas. Here is what each one does.

![The Website Builder interface showing the canvas, toolbar, and panel rail](../images/website-builder/builder-overview.png)

### The Canvas (Center)

The large area in the center of the screen is the **Canvas**. This is a live preview of your page, rendered with your real theme -- your fonts, your colors, dark mode and all -- so what you see is what your visitors get.

You can:

- Click on any element to select it
- Click on text to start editing it
- Drag components from the **Add** panel onto the Canvas

The Canvas updates instantly as you make changes.

The Builder opens ready to edit both your page **and** its shared header and footer, so you can fix a nav link or a footer address without switching anywhere else.

**Canvas View.** Click the canvas icon in the top toolbar to switch into Canvas View, where you can put several pages side by side as frames, pan between them, and zoom in and out. Edit any frame in place. Anything you change on a shared header or footer updates across every frame at once. Hold Cmd (Ctrl on Windows) and scroll to zoom; scroll normally to pan.

### The Top Toolbar

The toolbar along the top of the screen holds your main controls:

**On the left:**
- **Edit mode** -- Choose whether you are editing the **Page**, its **Layout** (the shared header/footer), or **Unlocked** (everything at once)
- **Undo and Redo**
- **Canvas View** -- Toggle the multi-page pan-and-zoom canvas

**In the middle:**
- **Device buttons** -- Mobile, Tablet, Laptop, Desktop, and Auto, for previewing your page at different widths
- **Breakpoint picker** and a custom width box, for finer control

**On the right:**
- **Save status** -- A small pill that reads Saved, Unsaved, or Saving…, so you always know where your work stands
- **Who else is here** -- Avatars of any teammates editing the same page with you
- **Preview** -- Hides all the editing chrome so you can see the page as a visitor would
- **Save** -- Saves your work as a draft
- **Publish** -- Makes your changes live

### The Panel Rail (Right)

Everything else lives in a single column of icon buttons down the **right** side of the screen. Each one opens a panel:

| Panel | What it is for |
|-------|---------------|
| **Pages** | Switch between pages, create new ones, and open a page's settings |
| **Add** | The component palette -- every building block you can drag onto your page |
| **Settings** | Options for whatever you currently have selected |
| **Styles** | Colors, spacing, size, fonts, borders, shadows, and layout for the selected element |
| **Layers** | A tree view of everything on the page, useful for selecting things buried inside other things |
| **Audit** | Scores your page on performance, accessibility, best practices, and SEO before you publish |
| **Builder settings** | Your own preferences -- canvas overlays, guide colors, default measurement unit |

Hover a rail button to peek at its panel; click it to pin the panel open so the canvas makes room for it. Click the pinned button again to go back to peeking.

> **Tip:** **Settings** and **Styles** need something selected before they have anything to show. If they look greyed out, click an element on your page first.

## How the Builder Works

Building a page follows a simple pattern. Here is the basic workflow:

1. **Pick a component** from the **Add** panel. Browse the categories to find what you need (a text block, an image, a button, and so on).
2. **Drag it onto your page.** Drop it wherever you want it to appear on the Canvas.
3. **Click to select and edit.** Click on the component you just added. If it has text, click the text to start typing. If it is an image, click it to change the picture.
4. **Style it in the Styles panel.** With the component selected, open **Styles** to adjust colors, spacing, fonts, and other visual settings.
5. **Save and publish.** When you are happy with your changes, click **Save** to store your work. When you are ready for the world to see it, click **Publish**.

That is the entire process. Pick, place, edit, style, publish.

---

## Switching Between Pages

You do not need to leave the Builder to work on a different page.

1. Click **Pages** in the panel rail on the right.
2. You will see every page on your site.
3. Click the page you want to switch to.

The Builder will load that page so you can start editing it right away.

You can also create a page without leaving the Builder: use the **New page** button at the top of the Pages panel, or the shortcuts on each page row to add a sub-page under it or open its settings (rename, layout, menus, SEO). Any unsaved canvas work is saved first, and after creating you land straight on the new page.

> **Tip:** In Canvas View you can skip switching altogether -- add several pages as frames and edit them side by side.

## Saving vs. Publishing

There is an important difference between saving and publishing:

### Saving (Draft)

- When you click **Save**, your changes are stored safely but are **not visible to your website visitors**.
- Think of it as saving a draft. You can come back later, make more changes, and save again as many times as you like.
- The Builder also saves for you: a couple of seconds after you stop editing, your work is saved automatically in the background. The save pill in the toolbar tells you where things stand.
- Drafts belong to the site, not to you personally -- teammates with builder access see the same draft, and can edit the same page alongside you.

### Publishing (Live)

- When you click **Publish**, your changes go live. **Your visitors will see the updated page** on your website.
- Only publish when you are confident your page looks the way you want.
- You can save many times before you publish. Publishing takes everything you have saved and makes it visible to the world.

> **Tip:** Press Cmd+S (Ctrl+S on Windows) any time you want to save immediately rather than waiting for the automatic save.

---

## Working With Teammates

You and your teammates can edit the same page at the same time. You will see each other's cursors, text selections, and typing live, and everyone's changes merge without anyone overwriting anyone else. Avatars in the top toolbar show who else is currently on the page.

Undo only ever undoes **your own** changes -- pressing Cmd+Z will never roll back a teammate's work.

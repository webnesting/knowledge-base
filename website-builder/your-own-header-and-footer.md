# Your own header and footer

**Last verified:** 2026-11-22 9:40pm

Your site's header and footer usually use one of the layouts in **Site Settings → Appearance → Header & Footer**. You can build your own header or footer in the builder and show it on every page instead. This guide covers making one, changing it, and going back to a standard layout.

Visitors see only the published version of your own header or footer. Changes you save but do not publish stay in the builder.

## Make your own header from your layout

The quickest start is a copy of the header you have now.

1. Open the builder and select the header at the top of your page.
2. In the bar on the header, select **Make It My Own**.
3. Select **Make My Header** to confirm.

You get a new header named after your site, for example "Acme header". It holds **Site logo**, **Site menu** and **Menu button** components laid out like your current layout. It opens in the builder and you can change anything in it. Your site keeps showing your layout until you publish the header with **Publish & Set as Header**.

Select your footer instead to make your own footer. The copy keeps everything your footer layout shows: your logo and footer text, your social links, your footer details, your footer menus and their titles, your copyright line and your extra footer text. The social links, footer details, copyright line, extra footer text, text under the logo and button are **Site details** components. The social links still change when you change them in **Site Settings**. The footer details, extra text, text under the logo and button keep the words you typed in the footer's **Settings**. The copyright line always shows this year.

The copy keeps what you set on your header or footer. A part you turned on is in the copy, and a part you turned off is not. The parts are the button, the social links, the top bar, and the language or light and dark switch. The logo choice, the menus you picked and how many menu levels show carry over too. The words (site name, button, top bar text) stay in your header's **Settings**, so they stay the same.

You need permission to make synced sections to make your own header or footer. **Publish & Set as Header** also needs permission to edit Site Settings.

## Change your header on your page

In the builder, your header and footer have a dashed outline, because they show on every page. Until you first select one, a hint under the top bar says so.

To pick another layout:

1. Select your header. **Settings** shows the layout it uses.
2. In **Settings**, pick a **Layout**. The page shows it at once, and **Save Layout** shows under the header's toolbar.
3. Select **Save Layout**. Every page shows the new layout.

To work on your own header, use **Make It My Own**, or **Edit Header** on a header of your own. The header opens right on your page:

- A blue ring marks the header, and the rest of the page is dimmed with the note "Locked while you edit the header". Clicks on the page do nothing until you close the header.
- Select a component in the header to change it in **Settings** and **Styles**. Drag new components from **Add** into the header.
- **Layers** lists only the header's components.
- The top bar's **Publish** and edit-mode switch are off while the header is open. The header's own toolbar has **Publish & Set as Header** (or **Publish**, once it is your header) and **Close**. **Close** keeps your changes as a draft that visitors do not see.

If a publish fails, a red notice above the page says so, nothing changes on your site, and the header stays open so you can try again.

When your own header is in use, select it to see its toolbar. **Edit Header** opens it on the page again, and **Go Back to a Standard Layout** shows your layout again. You cannot select the components inside your header from the page itself, because a click selects the whole header. In **Layers**, your header and footer show as **Header:** and **Footer:** with their name and a lock.

Header and footer widgets have their own group in **Add**, **Header & Footer Widgets**. They never go on a page. Select your header or footer to use one.

Footers work the same way.

## Publish your own header

A header made for your site has a different publish button in the builder:

1. Open the builder and select your header, then select **Edit Header**.
2. Add and arrange what you want: your logo, links, a button, a notice.
3. Select **Publish & Set as Header** in the header's toolbar.

Your header is published and every page shows it at the top, in one step. If something goes wrong, nothing changes on your site. After that, the button says **Publish**, and each publish updates your header on every page. A footer works the same way, with **Publish & Set as Footer**.

You can also ask an AI tool that you connect to WebNesting to build a header or footer and set it for you.

Something must be published before a header or footer can be used. An empty one is refused, so your pages never show an empty header.

## See and change your own header in Site Settings

Open **Site Settings → Appearance → Header & Footer**. While your own header is in use, the **Header** part shows:

- **Your own header** with an **In use** badge, and its name.
- When it was published. If you saved changes and did not publish them, it says how many, and that visitors still see the published version.
- A picture of your header as visitors see it.
- **Edit on My Home Page**, which opens the builder on your home page with your header selected.

The layout pictures and the layout choices are hidden while your own header is in use.

While your header uses a layout, the **Header** part starts with **Change it on your page** and **Edit on My Home Page**. Under the footer layouts, **Edit on My Home Page** opens your home page with the footer selected, so you can make a footer of your own there.

## Go back to a standard layout

1. Open **Site Settings → Appearance → Header & Footer**.
2. Under **Header**, select **Go Back to a Standard Layout**.
3. Select **Use** and the layout's name to confirm.

Every page shows the layout again at once. Your own header is kept, so you can use it again later. The footer works the same way. You can also do this from your header's bar in the builder.

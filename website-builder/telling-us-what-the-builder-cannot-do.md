# How to Tell Us What the Builder Can't Do

**Last verified:** 2026-11-02 8:38pm

Sometimes the Builder has no block, setting or theme option for what you want. Then you, or the AI app you connected, may add your own code to get it: your own style rules, a Style, or an HTML component. That works, but your page now depends on code you have to look after yourself.

We want to know each time this happens, because each report tells us what to build next. When the Builder can do it, we tell you, and you can swap your own code for the new built-in part.

## Before you start

- **If you [connected an AI tool](../dashboard/connecting-ai-tools.md),** you do not have to do anything. Your AI sends the report by itself (see below).
- **If you added the code yourself,** or used an AI app that is not connected to WebNesting, send the report with **Send Feedback** (see the steps).

### What your connected AI app does

When your AI adds its own code, or cannot do what you asked with the Builder's own parts, it sends us a short report. Then it tells you that it sent the report.

The report says:

- what you asked for,
- what your AI built instead, or that it could not do it,
- why the Builder could not do it,
- the names of the style rules, Styles and HTML components your AI made.

WebNesting adds these details itself, and your AI cannot change them:

- your name and email,
- the workspace, the site and the page,
- the name of your AI app, as it connected to WebNesting,
- the AI model, as your AI app reports it.

We also check whether each item your AI names is really on your site.

The report goes only to WebNesting's own help desk. It never goes into your workspace, your Help Desk or your site, and it does not change your pages. Your AI can send up to 10 reports an hour for you. If it has more, it puts several in one report.

Do not want reports sent? Tell your AI not to send them.

## Steps

To send the report yourself:

1. Click the **⋮** menu in the top bar.
2. Click **Send Feedback…**.
3. Pick **I have an idea**.
4. In the message, write the four things below.
5. Click **Send**.

What to write:

- **What I wanted:** for example, "a pricing table with the middle plan highlighted".
- **What I did instead:** for example, "an HTML component with my own table".
- **Why:** for example, "no block has a highlighted column".
- **Where:** the site and the page, and the names of the style rules, Styles or HTML components you made.

## Check it worked

- **Send Feedback** says your message was sent. It goes to our help desk with your name and email.
- A connected AI app tells you in the chat that it sent WebNesting a report.

When the Builder can do what you asked, we send you a message. It names your page and the custom work on it. It also gives you a ready request to paste into your AI app. That request asks your AI to remove your own code and use the new built-in part instead.

## If something goes wrong

- **"You have sent too many" or a wait message.** You can send up to 10 messages an hour with **Send Feedback**, and your AI can send up to 10 reports an hour. Wait, or put several things in one message.
- **Your AI says the report could not be sent.** Nothing was saved. Send the same four things with **Send Feedback**.
- **Does a report cost anything?** No. Reports are free and never count toward your plan.
- **Can my AI report on someone else's site?** No. It can report only on the sites and workspaces you can open yourself, and the report always names you.

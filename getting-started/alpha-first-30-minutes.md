# Your First 30 Minutes (Alpha Checklist)

**Last verified:** 2026-10-27 9:44am

WebNesting is in alpha. This checklist shows what you should be able to do in your first 30 minutes. Work through it in order. If a step does not work, or takes longer than it should, that is a finding: tell us with **Send feedback** (see the last section).

You are done when you can tick all six boxes.

---

## The checklist

1. **Sign up and verify your email.** Create your account, open the email we send you, and click the link. You land in your workspace.
   *Done when:* you see your workspace home and no error messages. More in [Creating Your Account](../account/creating-your-account.md).

2. **Create a site.** Open **Sites**, click **Create Site**, type a site name, and click **Create Site** again.
   *Done when:* your new site appears in the **Sites** list with its free `.webnesting.site` address.

3. **Pick a look.** In **Your look** on the same form, choose a theme before you create the site. If the theme comes with an example site, keep it on. You can change the look later under **Appearance**.
   *Done when:* the new site shows the pages and colors of the theme you picked. More in [Setting Up Your Site](setting-up-your-site.md).

4. **Edit a page.** Open **Website Builder**, click any text on your homepage, and type a change.
   *Done when:* the save pill in the toolbar says **Saved**. More in [Quick Start Guide](quick-start-guide.md).

5. **Publish.** Click the logo icon in the top-left corner of the builder, then **Publish**. Open your site address in a new tab.
   *Done when:* the live site shows your change.

6. **Preview your bill.** Go to **Billing** and open the **Cost calculator** tab. It starts from what is on in your workspace and shows the estimated cost for each month.
   *Done when:* you can see what your workspace costs per month and which line the cost comes from. More in [Billing and Usage](../dashboard/billing-and-usage.md).

---

## What to expect

- WebNesting is an alpha. Some things are rough, and some things change from week to week.
- Your usage bill follows the published rates. The **Cost calculator** shows an estimate, and your real bill is final on the 1st of the next month.
- See what we already know about, and what changed lately, on the [What's new and known issues](https://webnesting.app/whats-new) page.

## Tell us what went wrong

Click the **⋮** menu in the top bar, then **Send Feedback…**. Pick what it is about (something is not working, an idea, or a question), say which step you were on and what you expected, and click **Send**. Your message goes to our helpdesk. The box works on every screen. Before you write, you can check the [known issues and recent changes](https://webnesting.app/whats-new).

# Creating a Website

**Last verified:** 2026-05-11 9:00am

Ready to build your first site? This guide walks you through creating a new website on WebNesting.

---

## Where to Create a Site

Sites live inside a workspace. To create a new site:

1. Go to your **account portal** and open the **Workspaces** card.
2. Pick the workspace you want the site to belong to (or create a new workspace first).
3. Inside the workspace, click **Sites** in the sidebar.
4. Click the **Create Site** button.

---

## The Site Creation Form

You'll see a short form with a few fields to fill in.

### Choosing a site name

Pick a name for your site. Your site name can include letters, numbers, and spaces. This name becomes part of your web address. For example, if you choose "my-bakery," your site will be at:

**my-bakery.webnesting.site**

> **Tip:** Keep your site name short and easy to remember. You can always connect a custom domain later if you want a different web address.

### Setting your timezone

Choose the timezone where you're located. This makes sure dates and times across your site are displayed correctly.

### Connecting a custom domain (optional)

If you already own a domain name (like mybakery.com), you can connect it during setup. This is completely optional. You can always add a custom domain later.

---

## What Happens After You Click Create

Once you submit the form:

1. Your site begins setting up. This may take a moment.
2. A spinner shows on screen while everything is being prepared.
3. When it's done, your site is ready to use.

---

## Your Site Is Ready

Once your site has been created, you'll see it in your workspace's **Sites** list. From there, you have two options:

- **Click the admin panel link** to go to your site's admin dashboard. This is where you'll manage content, design your pages, and adjust settings.
- **Click the web address** to see your live site as visitors will see it.

> **Tip:** Your site is live right away, but it starts with a blank slate. Head to the admin dashboard to start adding content and making it your own.

---

## Setting Up Your Site for the First Time

When you open your site's admin dashboard for the first time, a setup wizard will guide you through the basics. It helps you configure your site's name, description, and other essential settings.

---

## Managing Multiple Sites

You can create more than one website on WebNesting. Each site has its own admin dashboard, its own content, and its own web address. All sites within a workspace are listed under the workspace's **Sites** page.

To create another site, click **Create Site** again and follow the same steps.

---

## Deleting a Site

If you no longer need a website, you can delete it.

1. Open the workspace that contains the site.
2. Click **Sites** in the sidebar.
3. Find the site you want to delete.
4. Click the delete option for that site.
5. Confirm that you want to delete it.

**Warning:** Deleting a site removes all of its content and data permanently. This cannot be undone. Make sure you're certain before you delete.

# Creating a Website

**Last verified:** 2026-10-27 10:19am

Ready to build your first site? This guide walks you through creating a new website on WebNesting.

---

## Where to Create a Site

Sites live inside a workspace. To create a new site:

1. Go to your **account portal** and open the **Workspaces** card.
2. Pick the workspace you want the site to belong to (or create a new workspace first).
3. Inside the workspace, select **Sites** in the left icon rail, then select **Create site** (or **+ New Site** at the top right of the page).

You can also start from **Settings** > **Products**: find the **Websites** card and select **Create Site**.

> **New workspace?** Until a workspace has its first site, the **Websites** card under **Settings** > **Products** is the place to start. Once your first site is ready, the **Sites** button in the rail is there for everything after that.

### The quick way — from your workspace list

The page that lists your workspaces has a blue **Start with a website** banner at the top. Select **Create a website** and a short popup walks you through the same thing without leaving the page: pick which workspace the site belongs to (it asks only if you have more than one), fill in the details below, choose how to start, and watch it set up. When it's ready, an **Open Site Admin** button takes you straight into your new site.

---

## The Site Creation Form

The form has three parts: the site details, how you want to start, and the look.

### Choosing a site name

Enter a name in **Site Name** -- this is what you'll see in your site list and in the builder. It can include letters, numbers, and spaces, for example "My Bakery".

### Your site address

As you type the name, **Site address** fills itself in (lowercase letters, numbers, and underscores) and the form shows the exact address your site will get, for example:

**my-bakery.webnesting.site**

You can change the address before creating the site; if you do and want it to follow the name again, select **Match the site name again**.

> **Tip:** Keep the address short and easy to remember. You can always connect a custom domain later if you want a different web address.

### Connecting a custom domain (optional)

If you already own a domain name (like mybakery.com), you can type it in **Custom Domain (optional)** during setup. This is completely optional. You can always connect one later; see [Connecting a Custom Domain](../getting-started/custom-domains.md).

### How you want to start, and the look

Under **How do you want to start?** you pick **Start from a theme**, **Bring over an existing page** (marked **Coming soon**) or **Build it with your AI**. If you start from a theme, **Pick a look** shows each theme drawn with your site name. [Setting Up Your Site](../getting-started/setting-up-your-site.md) explains each choice.

---

## What Happens After You Select Create Site

Once you select **Create Site** (**Create Website** in the popup):

1. Your site begins setting up. This takes about a minute.
2. A progress list shows each step, from **Creating folders** to **Applying your look**, with a tick beside each step that is done.
3. If you started from a theme, a **Make it yours** screen opens next. Pick your colors, fonts, header layout and light or dark mode. Your new home page changes beside the choices. Click **Done** to save them, or **Skip for Now** to change them later in **Site Settings**, under **Appearance**.
4. The page then says **Complete!** and shows a **Go to Sites** button.

---

## Your Site Is Ready

Select **Go to Sites**. Your new site is on the **Sites** page, which shows one card for each site with two buttons:

- **Manage Site** opens your site's admin dashboard. This is where you'll manage content, design your pages, and adjust settings.
- **View Site** shows your live site as visitors will see it.

> **Tip:** Your site is live right away. Its look is the one you picked, and its home page is empty unless you chose a theme with an example site or starter pages. Open **Manage Site**, then **Website Builder**, to start adding your own content.

## Setting Up Your Site for the First Time

See [Setting Up Your Site](../getting-started/setting-up-your-site.md) for each choice in the form and what to do after your site is ready: your logo, your favicon, your site name and your theme.

## Managing Multiple Sites

You can create more than one website on WebNesting. Each site has its own admin dashboard, its own content, and its own web address. All sites within a workspace are listed under the workspace's **Sites** page.

To create another site, select **+ New Site** on the **Sites** page and follow the same steps.

---

## Deleting a Site

You can't delete a site yourself yet. To remove one, email Support at support@webnesting.com and name the site. See [Managing Your Websites](managing-your-websites.md#deleting-a-website).

# Creating Your Account

**Last verified:** 2026-10-27 10:10am

Welcome to WebNesting! Getting started takes just a few minutes. This guide walks you through creating your account and getting ready to build your first website.

---

## Signing Up

1. Go to [webnesting.app](https://webnesting.app).
2. On the sign-in page, select **Create an account** under the **Sign in** button. (Starting from [WebNesting.com](https://webnesting.com) works too -- it sends you to the same place.)
3. Fill in the registration form:
   - **First name** -- Your first name.
   - **Last name** -- Your last name.
   - **Email** -- The email address you want to use for your account.
   - **Password** -- Choose a password of at least 8 characters. A note under the field tells you how many characters are left and how to make it stronger with numbers, capitals or symbols.
   - **Confirm password** -- Enter the same password again.
4. Optionally, check **Send me product updates and tips** if you'd like occasional emails about new features. It's unchecked by default, and you can change your mind anytime from your account portal.
5. If you have a promotion code for new accounts, find **Promotion code**, type the code and select **Check code**. You see what the code gives you. To use another code, select **Use a different code**.
6. Select **Create Account**.

> **Note:** If the email address you entered is already registered, you will see a message letting you know an account already exists. You can sign in instead or use a different email.

That's it! You're now logged in and taken to your account portal.

> **About promotion codes:** WebNesting keeps a sign-up code for the first workspace you create. The Create Workspace page shows it again, with what it gives you, before you create the workspace. A sign-up code works on one workspace only. If the page says the code is not valid, check the spelling. A code that is meant for an existing workspace is entered on that workspace's Billing page instead (see [Billing and Usage](../dashboard/billing-and-usage.md#promotion-codes)).

> **Tip:** Use an email address you check regularly. You'll need it to verify your account and receive important updates.

## Verifying Your Email Address

Before you can create a website, you need to verify your email address. This confirms that you own the email you signed up with.

### How to verify

1. Check your email inbox for a message from WebNesting.
2. Open the email and click the **verification link** inside it.
3. You'll be taken back to WebNesting, and your email is now verified.

### Didn't receive the email?

- Wait a minute or two. Sometimes emails take a moment to arrive.
- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Make sure you entered the correct email address when you signed up.
- Select **Resend Verification Email** on your account portal. The page shows it above the cards until your email is verified.

> **Tip:** If you still can't find the email after checking your spam folder, try adding WebNesting to your email contacts and requesting the verification again.

---

## What You See After Verification

Once your email is verified, you'll land on your **account portal**. This is your home base for managing personal account details. You'll see a few cards on this page:

- **Profile** -- Your personal information.
- **Security** -- Your password, two-factor authentication, and where you're signed in.
- **Workspaces** -- Open one of your workspaces. Workspaces are where your websites, billing, usage, and team members live.

You're all set! Select **Create Your First Workspace** to create your first workspace and site.

When you create a workspace, the **Create Workspace** page has a **Promotion code** section:

- If you entered a code when you signed up, a notice names the code and what it gives you. It is applied to this workspace when you create it.
- To add a code for this workspace, select **Have a code?** (or **Have another code?**), type the code and select **Check code**. You see what it gives you. The code is applied when you select **Create Workspace**.

After the workspace is ready, a message names each code that was applied.

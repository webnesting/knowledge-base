# Closing Your Workspace or Account

**Last verified:** 2026-10-27 10:11am

This guide shows how to save what you want to keep, and how to ask Support to close a workspace or your account.

You cannot close a workspace or an account yourself yet. **Settings** > **General** says so under **Danger Zone**: to delete or transfer a workspace, contact Support.

---

## Before you start

Closing removes your sites, pages, files and contact data. Do these first.

1. **Pay what you owe.** Open **Settings** > **Plans & Billing**. Pay any outstanding invoice with **Pay Now** or **Pay All Now**.
2. **Save your contacts.** Open **Contacts** and select **Export CSV**.
3. **Save form submissions.** Open each form's **Submissions** tab and export the list.
4. **Save your files.** Open **Files** in the top bar and download what you want to keep.
5. **Save a product's data.** For Marketing, Helpdesk or Tasks, turn the product off, then select **Download a copy** on its card. WebNesting emails you a link that works for 7 days. See [Workspace Products](../dashboard/workspace-products.md#disabling-a-product).
6. **Tell your team.** Everyone in the workspace loses access.

> **Warning:** Closing cannot be undone. If you are not sure, keep the workspace. A workspace with nothing turned on costs nothing beyond what you use.

---

## Ask Support to close it

1. Email Support at support@webnesting.com from the email address on your account.
2. Say whether you want to close one workspace or your whole account.
3. Give the workspace name.
4. Say that you have saved what you need.

---

## What is kept and for how long

- **A product you turn off.** Its data is permanently deleted 30 days after it turns off, unless you turn it back on. **Delete now** deletes it at once.
- **Backups.** WebNesting keeps backups of its databases for 30 days. A deletion reaches the backups when that time passes.
- **Your final invoice.** Charges up to the day of closing are still billed. See [Billing and Usage](../dashboard/billing-and-usage.md).

---

## If something goes wrong

- **You closed too early and want something back.** Email Support right away. A product's data is only kept for the first 30 days.
- **You only want fewer charges.** Turn off the products and modules you do not use instead. See [Workspace Products](../dashboard/workspace-products.md) and [Modules and Features](../dashboard/modules-and-features.md).

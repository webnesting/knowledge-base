# Managing Your Websites

**Last verified:** 2026-10-27 10:10am

The **Sites** page inside a workspace is where you manage all your sites. This guide covers everything you need to know about viewing, opening, editing, and deleting your websites.

---

## Viewing All Your Websites

1. Open your **account portal** and click the **Workspaces** card.
2. Pick the workspace that contains the sites you want to manage.
3. Select **Sites** in the left icon rail.

You'll see one card for every website in that workspace. Each card shows:

- **The site name** -- The name you gave your site.
- **The web address** -- Where your site lives (for example, my-bakery.dev.webnesting.site while you try WebNesting out).
- **When it was last updated** -- For example, "Updated Oct 27, 2026".
- **View Site** and **Manage Site** buttons.

Select **Filters** at the top right and pick a status (**Active**, **Provisioning** or **Failed**) to narrow the list.

---

## Opening Your Site's Admin Dashboard

The admin dashboard is where you manage content, change the design, and adjust settings for a specific site.

1. Find the site you want to manage in the workspace **Sites** list.
2. Select **Manage Site** on its card.
3. You'll be taken directly to the admin dashboard.

You're automatically signed in. There's no need to enter your email and password again.

> **Tip:** Bookmark your site's admin dashboard if you visit it often. It saves you a step.

---

## Visiting Your Live Site

Want to see what your site looks like to visitors?

1. Find the site in the workspace **Sites** list.
2. Select **View Site** on its card.
3. Your live site opens in a new tab.

This is exactly what your visitors see when they come to your site.

---

## Editing Site Details

Open the site with **Manage Site**, then open **Settings** > **Site Settings**. **Site Configuration** holds the site's name, web address, visibility and timezone. **SEO Defaults** holds the title and description search engines show. See [Site Settings](../dashboard/site-settings.md).

---

## Deleting a Website

You can't delete a site yourself yet: the **Sites** page has no delete button. To remove a site, email Support at support@webnesting.com. Name the site and the workspace it is in.

**Warning:** Deleting a website removes all of its content, pages, images, and settings permanently. This action cannot be undone. Download anything you want to keep first. For form submissions, use the CSV export on each form's **Submissions** tab. For a module's content, use **Download a copy** on its card under **Settings** > **Modules**.

> **Tip:** If you're unsure, keep the site. You can ask Support to remove it later. To give a site to someone else, see [Moving a Site to Someone Else](moving-a-site-to-someone-else.md). To close a whole workspace, see [Closing Your Workspace or Account](closing-your-account.md).

---

## Creating Additional Websites

You can create as many websites as you need within a workspace. Each one has its own admin dashboard, its own content, and its own web address.

To create a new site:

1. Open the workspace and select **Sites** in the left icon rail.
2. Select **+ New Site** (or **Create site** in the side menu).
3. Follow the steps in the creation form.

---

## Tips for Managing Multiple Sites

- **Use clear, descriptive site names.** If you have several sites, distinct names help you quickly find the one you need.
- **Check usage by site.** The workspace **Usage** page lists your pages for each website, so you can see which sites use the most.
- **Keep your sites organized.** Visit your workspace **Sites** page regularly to review your sites. Ask Support to remove any you no longer need.

> **Tip:** Each site operates independently. Changes you make to one site won't affect your others.

# Managing Your Websites

**Last verified:** 2026-05-11 9:00am

The **Sites** page inside a workspace is where you manage all your sites. This guide covers everything you need to know about viewing, opening, editing, and deleting your websites.

---

## Viewing All Your Websites

1. Open your **account portal** and click the **Workspaces** card.
2. Pick the workspace that contains the sites you want to manage.
3. Click **Sites** in the workspace sidebar.

You'll see a list of every website in that workspace. Each entry shows:

- **Site name** -- The name you gave your site.
- **Web address** -- The URL where your site lives (for example, my-bakery.webnesting.site).
- **Status** -- Whether your site is active, still provisioning, or in another state.
- **Date created** -- When you first created the site.

You can filter the list by status (Active, Provisioning, Failed) using the filter sidebar.

---

## Opening Your Site's Admin Dashboard

The admin dashboard is where you manage content, change the design, and adjust settings for a specific site.

1. Find the site you want to manage in the workspace **Sites** list.
2. Click the admin panel link (labeled with your site's admin path, such as **Sandbox**) next to that site.
3. You'll be taken directly to the admin dashboard.

You're automatically signed in. There's no need to enter your email and password again.

> **Tip:** Bookmark your site's admin dashboard if you visit it often. It saves you a step.

---

## Visiting Your Live Site

Want to see what your site looks like to visitors?

1. Find the site in the workspace **Sites** list.
2. Click the **web address** for that site.
3. Your live site opens in a new tab.

This is exactly what your visitors see when they come to your site.

---

## Editing Site Details

You can update basic information about your site after it's been created.

1. Find the site in the workspace **Sites** list.
2. Click the option to edit the site's details.
3. Make your changes.
4. Save your updates.

---

## Deleting a Website

If you no longer need a site, you can delete it. Please read this section carefully before proceeding.

1. Find the site in the workspace **Sites** list.
2. Click the delete option for that site.
3. You'll be asked to confirm your decision.
4. Confirm the deletion.

**Warning:** Deleting a website removes all of its content, pages, images, and settings permanently. This action cannot be undone. Be absolutely certain before you delete a site.

> **Tip:** If you're unsure, consider keeping the site rather than deleting it. You can always delete it later.

---

## Creating Additional Websites

You can create as many websites as you need within a workspace. Each one has its own admin dashboard, its own content, and its own web address.

To create a new site:

1. Open the workspace and click **Sites** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Create Site**.
3. Follow the steps in the creation form.

---

## Tips for Managing Multiple Sites

- **Use clear, descriptive site names.** If you have several sites, distinct names help you quickly find the one you need.
- **Check usage by site.** The workspace **Usage** page breaks down resource usage per site, so you can see which sites are using the most.
- **Keep your sites organized.** Visit your workspace **Sites** page regularly to review your sites and remove any you no longer need.

> **Tip:** Each site operates independently. Changes you make to one site won't affect your others.
